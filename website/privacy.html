Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

  1. Who we are

    Rewrite Labs is a brand of Cast Iron Productions LLC. Cast Iron Productions LLC runs this site and is responsible for the information described here. Its address is 7901 4th St N, Ste 300, St. Petersburg, FL 33702.

  2. What we collect

    This site has two forms: the early-access waitlist, and the newsletter signup on the blog and AI News Feed. They are separate lists. When you submit either one, we store:

    • your email address
    • the date and time you signed up
    • your country, as reported by Cloudflare from your connection

    The newsletter form also stores the tracks you pick (Operator Notes, Field Notes, or both) and the page you signed up from. We do not ask for your name or any other detail.

  3. How we use it

    We use a waitlist address only to contact you about early access to Rewrite Labs. We use a newsletter address only to send the tracks you picked. Signing up for one never adds you to the other.

  4. Where it is stored

    Newsletter emails are sent through Kit (kit.com), our email service provider. Kit receives your address and the tracks you picked, sends the email that confirms your signup, and records whether newsletter emails are opened and which links are clicked.

    Both lists are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV. This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes requests to the site and, like any host, handles visitors’ IP addresses to deliver pages.

  5. Cookies and analytics

    This site does not set cookies. It does not run analytics or advertising trackers. If you pick a light or dark theme, your browser remembers that choice on your device. We never receive it.

    Fonts and scripts load from rewritelabs.io itself, so no other company sees your visit.

  6. Your requests

    Every newsletter email has an unsubscribe link. To ask what we hold about you, or to have your waitlist entry or newsletter subscription removed, email info@rewritelabs.io. Send the request from the address you signed up with.

  7. Changes

    If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
