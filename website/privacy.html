Rewrite Labs is a brand of Cast Iron Productions LLC. Cast Iron Productions LLC runs this site and is responsible for the information described here. Its address is 7901 4th St N, Ste 300, St. Petersburg, FL 33702.

What we collect

The only form on this site is the early-access waitlist. When you submit it, we store:

How we use it

We use your email address only to contact you about early access to Rewrite Labs.

Where it is stored

The waitlist is stored in Cloudflare Workers KV. This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes requests to the site and, like any host, handles visitors’ IP addresses to deliver pages.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set cookies. It does not run analytics or advertising trackers. If you pick a light or dark theme, your browser remembers that choice on your device. We never receive it.

Fonts and scripts load from rewritelabs.io itself, so no other company sees your visit.

Your requests

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your waitlist entry removed, email info@rewritelabs.io. Send the request from the address you signed up with.
