A sample redesign, built by our AI.

This is an illustrative example, not a real client. Sunbelt Comfort HVAC is a fictional business, and every name, phone number, and claim on this page is invented. It shows the kind of before-and-after our pipeline aims to produce: a cluttered nine-item nav, six repeated estimate buttons, and keyword-stuffed paragraphs become a focused, scannable page. No real business is shown and no results are claimed.

What our AI changed

BeforeAfter
−Nine-item flat navigation, three levels of dropdowns+Four links, one always-visible phone CTA
−“Get an estimate” repeated six times down the page+One persistent call-to-action, styled to stand out once
−Generic stock photography with no captions+Sized, purposeful imagery tied to each service
−Dense, keyword-stuffed paragraphs written for search engines+Short, scannable copy written for a homeowner with a broken AC
−No trust signals above the fold+Years in business, accreditations, and license number surfaced in the hero
