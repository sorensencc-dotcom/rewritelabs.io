### 7. Core Pipeline + SOPs

The Rewrite Labs core pipeline follows a deterministic 4-phase process:

1. Discover
2. Redesign
3. Outreach
4. Deliver

Each phase includes specific SOPs, tools, and operator responsibilities.

---

#### 7.1 Discover Phase
- Identify potential leads via scraping, referrals, or inbound.
- Validate lead quality using scoring rubric.
- Enrich leads with metadata and contact info.

#### 7.2 Redesign Phase
- Run automated Lighthouse audits.
- Generate redesigns using AI design engine.
- Produce before/after visual diffs.
- Store redesigns in client folders.

#### 7.3 Outreach Phase
- Generate personalized messaging.
- Send outreach emails in deterministic cadence.
- Track engagement and responses.

#### 7.4 Deliver Phase
- Onboard converted clients.
- Deliver final assets and documentation.
- Collect feedback and testimonials.

#### 7.5 SOPs
- Daily standup at 9:00 AM EST.
- Weekly roadmap review on Fridays.
- All client data stored in Notion + Google Drive.

---

### 7.6 Outreach Automation System — End‑to‑End Specification

**Purpose**  
Automate the full Outreach phase of the Rewrite Labs pipeline using deterministic, AI‑driven systems that generate personalized messaging, manage multi‑step cadences, and sync with CRM infrastructure.

**Scope**  
This subsystem activates after Redesign output is finalized and approved. It consumes redesign diffs, Lighthouse data, and brand‑tone profiles to generate personalized outreach sequences and manage delivery.

---

#### 7.6.1 System Components

**Lead Research Engine**  
- Extracts business metadata, site structure, social presence, and contact info.  
- Produces structured lead profiles with scoring attributes.  
- Flags high‑value targets based on rubric (traffic, industry, redesign delta, responsiveness).

**Personalization Engine**  
- Generates personalized email sequences using redesign diffs, value‑gap summaries, and brand‑tone alignment.  
- Produces before/after visual diffs and tailored value propositions.  
- Supports multi‑variant generation for A/B testing.

**Outreach Sequencer**  
- Deterministic cadence: Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 14.  
- Tracks opens, clicks, replies, and bounce events.  
- Auto‑pauses when a lead responds or converts.

**CRM Sync Layer**  
- Bi‑directional sync with HubSpot/Airtable/Sheets.  
- Logs lead status, outreach stage, engagement metrics, and conversion events.  
- Ensures a single source of truth across operator tools.

**Operator Console Integration**  
- Real‑time visibility into:  
  - Pending outreach  
  - Active sequences  
  - Errors  
  - Deliverability metrics  
- Manual override tools for pausing, editing, or re‑sequencing leads.

---

#### 7.6.2 Operational Flow

1. **Lead enters pipeline** (from Discover or manual import).  
2. **Lead Research Engine** generates structured profile.  
3. **Personalization Engine** produces outreach assets.  
4. **Outreach Sequencer** deploys cadence.  
5. **CRM Sync Layer** updates lead status continuously.  
6. **Operator Console** provides oversight and manual controls.  
7. **Conversion event** triggers handoff to Deliver phase.

---

#### 7.6.3 Operator Responsibilities

- Review high‑value leads flagged by scoring engine.  
- Approve or adjust personalized messaging when needed.  
- Monitor deliverability and error logs.  
- Trigger manual overrides for sensitive accounts.  
- Validate CRM sync integrity weekly.

---

#### 7.6.4 KPIs

- Lead‑to‑response rate  
- Response‑to‑conversion rate  
- Deliverability score  
- Personalization accuracy  
- Time‑to‑first‑response  
- Outreach throughput per day
