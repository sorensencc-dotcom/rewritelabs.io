1. **Zero-Knowledge Credential Brokering & Mutation Gates (`sigil` / `toolforge`):** The simultaneous launch of 1Password for Agents, bank blocks on unverified browser agents, and METR's discovery that OpenAI's turn-end monitors missed continuous agent action loops reinforce a single architectural rule: monitoring and credential injection must happen per-action at the harness boundary, never inside prompt instructions or only at turn completion.

3. **Code-First Content Architecture (`rewritelabs.io`):** Lee Robinson's $260 CMS-to-Markdown migration of `cursor.com` directly mirrors the `rewritelabs.io` static content-site architecture—eliminating headless CMS network boundaries so coding agents can grep, validate, and ship content PRs natively.
