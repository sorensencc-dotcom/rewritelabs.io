**Industry & Architecture Takeaways:**
1. **Enforce OS-Level Sandboxing & Least-Privilege Boundaries for Local Agents:** OpenAI's Codex sandbox design (read-only `.git` and config roots, domain-allowlisted network proxies, and wrapped MCP servers) and Meta Muse's permission-leak incidents show that prompt-level rules cannot contain compromised or over-privileged agents. Production agent runners should isolate execution at the OS/proxy layer and gate sensitive actions behind explicit approval.
2. **Design Agent Workflows Around the Jagged Frontier (46% vs. 25%):** Empirical data from OpenAI shows coding agents excel at monitoring runs (79%) and 2–4 hour scoped tasks (46%) while struggling on complex inference/serving stack engineering (25%). Engineering teams get the highest ROI by pairing multi-agent concurrency with human-in-the-loop checkpoints for architecture and verification.
3. **Budget for High-Concurrency Token Spend & Terminal Cost Visibility:** With median frontier researchers running $2,400/day in agentic compute and 10x annual effective compute growth driving rapid commoditization, teams should instrument per-workflow token telemetry and CLI billing checks (such as `vercel billing`) to prevent runaway spend.
