**Industry & Architecture Takeaways:**
1. **Replace Text Generation with Bounded Decision Models:** High-frequency agent steps (policy gating, tool routing, and triage verification) do not require verbose natural language output. Adopting non-generative decision models like Gemini Decisions API cuts routing latency to sub-150ms while driving evaluation costs down to $0.05 per million tokens with free outputs.
2. **Architect for Continuous Proactive Ingress:** The evolution from user-prompted assistants to background proactive agents (OpenAI Dots, Claude Duet) requires continuous, asynchronous observation of user communication and calendar streams. Production architectures must incorporate persistent state listeners that cross-reference incoming confirmations against prior commitments without waiting for explicit chat triggers.
3. **Build B2A Machine-Readable Product Endpoints:** As autonomous agents capture a growing share of consumer and enterprise purchasing (driving 13x annual order growth on Shopify), organizations must publish structured, machine-readable catalog schemas (`.well-known/ai-agent.json`, API parameter endpoints) to ensure their products are discoverable and actionable by buyer agents.
