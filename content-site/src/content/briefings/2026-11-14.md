**Industry & Architecture Takeaways:**
1. **Decouple Agent Control Planes, Credential Proxies, and Memory Provenance:** Production agent architectures are converging on split brain/hands isolation—keeping session history, orchestration loops, and credential vaults outside ephemeral execution sandboxes—while pairing pre-execution policy checks with behavioral anomaly detection. Because post-execution monitors still struggle with poisoned long-term memory (catching 4 of 18 cases in Google's benchmarks) and cold-start agents (<350 benign traces), teams must enforce strict provenance and screening on persistent memory writes.
2. **Replace Generative LLM Judges with Non-Generative Decision Models in High-Volume Triage:** Single-forward-pass decision models with bidirectional attention (like Perplexity Decider v1.3 at $26.47 per 1M multi-question requests) deliver higher routing, reranking, and span-extraction accuracy than general generative judges at a fraction of the cost and latency, while integer-only inference (DetLLM) points toward bit-exact reproducibility across heterogeneous hardware.
3. **Prepare Web Infrastructure for Signed Agentic Commerce:** As consumer and developer agents gain tokenized payment rails (ChatGPT Wallet and Replit Agent via Stripe Link/Visa), traditional bot-mitigation rules on CDNs and WAFs risk blocking legitimate purchases. Engineering teams should adopt cryptographic Web Bot Auth verification and structured WebMCP tool endpoints to separate authorized transactional agents from abusive scrapers.
