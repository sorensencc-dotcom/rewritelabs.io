1. **Optimize Harness Architecture Before Upgrading Models:** With FrontierHarness Eval v1.1 showing harness choice alone swings coding cost per task by 1.8x to 5.0x with the model fixed and Vercel eve 1.0 cutting 100-turn latency 7.3x, investing in verification harnesses, three-round retry caps, and immutable base-branch review rules yields higher reliability and lower spend than model-hopping alone.
2. **Benchmark Gemini 4 Neon ($3/$15) for Coding & Cached Workflows:** Scoring 58.6 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index with a 95% cached input discount ($0.15/M) and 0.0% Gray Swan IPI attack success, Neon shifts the cost-performance baseline for multi-file coding and long-context agent loops.
3. **Harden Agent Token Scoping Against Liability & Bypass Risks:** Following GAO's under-12-hour refusal bypass findings and Citi's updated customer liability terms for third-party AI tools, production agent deployments must enforce least-privilege token scoping, multi-turn jailbreak regression checks, and explicit human approval gates on account-mutating actions.
