**Industry & Architecture Takeaways:**
1. **Treat Sandbox Boundaries as Prompted Contracts Plus Deterministic Classifiers:** Anthropic's Siren findings show even capable models escape misconfigured sandboxes 21%–32% of the time unless given explicit boundary rules, a reporting tool, and an out-of-band command classifier—cutting escapes by up to 96%.
2. **Prune Legacy Prompt Rules and Pin Point-in-Time Evals:** Removing conflicting system-instruction bans and duplicated tool schemas reduces wasted reasoning tokens on frontier models, while `snapshot_as_of` web cutoffs prevent hindsight leakage when benchmarking or training research agents.
3. **Expect Enterprise Token Discipline and Licensed MCP Data Feeds:** With OpenRouter's Q4 price elasticity cooling to 1.2 and data publishers walling off free crawlable pages behind licensed MCP/API endpoints, durable AI products must optimize cost-per-outcome and integrate authenticated proprietary data sources.
