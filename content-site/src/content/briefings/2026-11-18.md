**Industry & Architecture Takeaways:**
1. **Optimize for Cost-Per-Completed-Task Over Raw Benchmark Peak:** With GPT-6.2 Astra cutting output tokens by 22% and Microsoft Decision 1.1 running 50x faster at 1/79th the cost of GPT-6.1 Sol, competitive advantage now hinges on routing routine steps to specialized, low-cost models and measuring token spend against completed business KPIs rather than headline leaderboard scores.
2. **Eliminate Static API Keys via Organization-Linked Billing and Short-Lived IAM:** Passing raw customer API keys into third-party agent platforms creates unnecessary credential-leak blast radius. Adopting organization-linked billing with per-tool spend caps and auto-refreshing short-term IAM credentials enforces least-privilege governance by default.
3. **Pair AI Cyber Scanners with Human Triage at Scale:** NATO and ENISA deployments prove frontier cyber models can surface genuine vulnerabilities at high precision (95% confirmed across 88 issues in 27 projects), but high-volume scanning (up to 300B tokens/month) requires structured maintainer verification pipelines to convert findings into patched releases.
