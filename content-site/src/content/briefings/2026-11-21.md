**Industry & Architecture Takeaways:**
1. **Defer Long-Tail Tool Schemas & Pair Agents With Authoritative Plugins:** Google's Gemini Tool Retrieval (`defer_loading: true`) preserves prefix caches while scaling to hundreds of tools, and Stripe's 69-scenario evals prove that providing an authoritative MCP/skills plugin cuts outdated API usage from 7% to 1.3% and web searches by 96%.
2. **Coordinate Multi-Agent Swarms via Shared Boards, Not Coordinator DMs:** Replit's Optimization-Bench shows that 32-agent swarms scale to 18.92x speedups only when workers claim independent tasks on a shared board (82% board traffic), whereas routing status through a central coordinator collapses performance once DM volume hits 600+ messages.
3. **Test Browser Agents in Hermetic Synthetic Environments:** Chrome's Mirror Dimension architecture (gVisor isolation, dynamic TLS leaf certs, and header-injected adversarial payloads) eliminates eval awareness and live-web side effects while multiplying red-team test throughput by 30x.
