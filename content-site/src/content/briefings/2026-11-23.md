**Industry & Architecture Takeaways:**
1. **Enforce Requirement-Level Checklists & Independent Verifiers on Coding Agents:** While formal Lean verification certified a 17M-line mathematical proof, SWE-CoWork proves that unverified workplace coding agents fail 31% of self-reported requirements and complete under 8% of multi-source assignments. Production coding harnesses should require explicit pre-edit requirement/acceptance checklists and independent post-edit verification.
2. **Audit Inference Routing Beyond First-Hop Zero Data Retention Flags:** OpenRouter's removal of 3 providers and 125 ZDR endpoints shows that reseller privacy flags do not guarantee downstream non-retention. Sensitive workloads should route directly to attested providers, self-host compact open-weight models like Inkling 2 Mini (~20GB), or inject canary tokens to detect leakage.
3. **Decouple Platform Margin From Falling Token Prices:** With token prices down 80% since January 2025 and open-weight usage surging, opaque credit markups create customer friction. Transparent pass-through or BYOK/linked-billing architectures paired with a clear orchestration or platform fee protect margins and lift conversion.
